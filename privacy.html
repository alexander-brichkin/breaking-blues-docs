Breaking Blues — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Short version: Breaking Blues does not collect, send or share your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores on your device

  • The people you add (name and the messaging apps you choose for them).
  • Your mood check-ins and optional notes, and your circle choices.
  • Your language and app settings.

This data is kept only in the app's local storage on your iPhone. We have no account system and no server that receives it. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Contacts (optional): used only to import people you pick. Only the contacts you select are read; nothing is uploaded.
  • Calendar (optional): read on your device to remind you to message someone before a planned meeting. Calendar data never leaves your device.

Messages

The app drafts a message and hands it to the messaging app you choose (for example Messages or WhatsApp). You always review and send it yourself. We never see or store the message.

Tracking, analytics and advertising

None. The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Not a medical service

Breaking Blues is not a medical device or a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis, use the "Need help now?" button in the app or contact your local emergency number.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: ab@agonist.se.
